What happens next

  1. We draw your pet as a coloring page and check it by hand.
  2. You get the page and its video by email within 3 days, usually the next day. A redo adds up to 2 days.
  3. Your pet takes a guest-star spot in Puppy Love, which closes January 10. The book always goes out on that date; our own pets fill the rest.
  4. The PDF book arrives within 7 days of the closing date.
  5. Paperbacks are printed on demand by Amazon (Kindle Direct Publishing) and arrive within 4 weeks of the closing date.
  6. Late on any of these? We make it up to you, for example with a free extra page.
