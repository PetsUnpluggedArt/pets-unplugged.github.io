Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Who We Are

Pets Unplugged is a brand of Kubbord Labs LLC, an Indiana limited liability company, operating at petsunplugged.com. We create interactive pet coloring pages and books with QR code animations. For privacy questions, contact us at hello@petsunplugged.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the site and serve you:

  • Email address: when you join our email list, join the "Your pet" waitlist, or ask us to tell you when a new book is out. These sign-ups are handled by Brevo.
  • Order details: when you buy a download or get a free page, Payhip collects your email address and, for paid orders, your payment details, so it can deliver your files. Amazon and Etsy do the same for orders placed with them.
  • Usage data: pages visited, time on site, browser type and general location (country or region), collected automatically by Google Analytics.

We do not collect or store payment card details. Payments are handled by Payhip, Amazon or Etsy, and we never see your card number.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Email address: to send the emails you asked for, such as news about new books and pages, free pages, the "Your pet" opening and offers. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in any email.
  • Order details: to deliver your downloads and help if something goes wrong with an order.
  • Analytics data: to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. We look at it in aggregate.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Services We Use

These services process data for us, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Payhip: checkout and downloads for our PDF book, single pages, bundles and free pages. Payhip Privacy Policy
  • Brevo: email sign-ups and the emails we send. Brevo's sign-up form uses Google reCAPTCHA to block spam. Brevo Privacy Policy
  • Vimeo: hosting for our coloring-page videos and QR code animations. Vimeo Privacy Policy
  • Amazon: the PAWS paperback. Amazon Privacy Policy
  • Etsy: our other listings, including printable pages and merch. Etsy Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics: website traffic statistics. Google Privacy Policy

5. Cookies

Our site uses cookies mainly through Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. The Vimeo player loads only when you press play, and Google reCAPTCHA loads only when you start filling in a sign-up form; both may set their own cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings, though some features may then not work.

6. Children's Privacy

Our pages are made for kids to color, but our sign-ups and shop are for grown-ups. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us their information, email hello@petsunplugged.com and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccurate data
  • Delete your data
  • Unsubscribe from our emails at any time
  • Opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on

To use any of these rights, email us at hello@petsunplugged.com.

8. Data Retention

We keep your email address for as long as you stay subscribed. Order records are kept by Payhip, Amazon or Etsy under their own rules. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
