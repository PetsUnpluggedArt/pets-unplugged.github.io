What to put in a notice

Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), your notice needs:

  • Your name, address, phone number and email address.
  • The work you own (for example a drawing, an illustration, an animation or a photo), or a link to it.
  • Where the copy is: the coloring page, animation, photo or Etsy listing, with its link or a screenshot.
  • A statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorized by you, your agent or the law.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.
  • Your physical or electronic signature (typing your full name is enough).

We remove or disable reported material promptly, and if someone sent it to us (for example a pet photo), we tell them. Knowingly claiming that something copies your work when it doesn't can make you liable for damages.

If your material was removed

If a photo or anything else you sent us was removed and you believe that was a mistake, send a counter-notice to the same agent with: your name, address, phone number and email address; what was removed and where it was; a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake or misidentification; your consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for your address (or, outside the United States, any judicial district where Kubbord Labs LLC may be found) and to accept service from the person who sent the notice; and your signature. We pass it to that person and may restore the material after 10 to 14 business days unless they tell us they have gone to court.

Repeat infringers

We stop accepting and featuring photos and other submissions from people who repeatedly send us other people's work, and we remove what they sent.
